Research Areas

Research Areas

From protocol design to real-world blockchain applications

Data Marketplaces

Energy Data Exchange

Decentralized data marketplaces for the energy domain, with a focus on privacy, trust, and interoperability using Ocean Protocol and GAIA-X.

Governance

DAO Sustainability

On-chain governance metrics, game-theoretic incentive models, and empirical analysis of what keeps DAOs healthy long-term.

Cryptography

Privacy-Preserving Techniques

Practical applications of ZK proofs, Multi-Party Computation, and Fully Homomorphic Encryption in blockchain systems.

Scaling

Layer 2 and Rollups

Rollup architectures, cross-layer composability, and L2 infrastructure for data marketplaces and DApps.

Foundations

Protocols and Consensus

Consensus mechanisms, protocol design trade-offs, and hands-on experience with Cosmos SDK and Hyperledger Fabric.
