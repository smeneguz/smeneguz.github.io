## Thesis Supervision

I co-supervise M.Sc. theses at **Politecnico di Torino**, in collaboration with the University of Turin (and, until 2026, Links Foundation). All theses below are open access on [Politecnico di Torino WebThesis](https://webthesis.biblio.polito.it/view/relators/Meneguzzo=3ASilvio=3A=3A.html).

### Looking for Thesis Students

I am currently looking for new M.Sc. thesis students. There are two ways to start:

- **Propose your own topic.** If you have a specific idea in blockchain, DLT, DAOs, applied cryptography or decentralised data markets, send me a short proposal with a description of your background.
- **Join my Ethereum Foundation project.** I have a thesis proposal ready, aligned with my active Ethereum Foundation research grant on [citizen-controlled energy data markets on Ethereum](/research/). Write to me and I will send you the details.

Other topics I propose are listed on the [contact page](/contact/#thesis-topics). Get in touch by [email](mailto:meneguzzosilvio@gmail.com).
