## 2026

<div class="news-item">
  <div class="news-date">Jul 2026</div>
  <div class="news-content">
    <h3>IEEE COMPSAC 2026, Madrid: BlockDLT 2nd Edition, Three Papers, and an IEEE Recognition</h3>
    <div class="news-summary">
      A packed edition of <a href="https://ieeecompsac.computer.org/2026/" target="_blank">IEEE COMPSAC 2026</a> (July 7-10, Universidad Politécnica de Madrid — the conference's 50th anniversary). I chaired the <a href="https://ieeecompsac.computer.org/2026/2nd-ieee-international-workshop-on-blockchain-and-distributed-ledger-technologies-blockdlt-2026/" target="_blank">2nd IEEE International Workshop on Blockchain and Distributed Ledger Technologies (BlockDLT 2026)</a>, co-organised the <a href="https://ieeecompsac.computer.org/2026/2nd-ieee-mentorium-workshop-building-blocks-for-early-career-researchers-mentorium-2026/" target="_blank">2nd IEEE Mentorium Workshop</a> and the <a href="https://ieeecompsac.computer.org/2026/srs-2026/" target="_blank">Student Research Symposium (SRS 2026)</a>, and presented three workshop papers: a dual-chain NFT architecture for automotive digital product passports (SESS), blockchain integrity verification overhead in energy digital twin pipelines (ICT4SMARTGRID), and <em>Portus</em>, an electronic bill of lading protocol on IOTA Move (SAPSE).
    </div>
    <div class="news-summary" style="margin-top:8px;">
      At the conference banquet I received an <strong>IEEE Computer Society Certificate of Appreciation</strong> for serving as COMPSAC 2026 Co-Chair of the Student Research Symposium.
